# Image credits

The photographs in this folder are stock placeholders used for demonstration listings only.
They are **not** photographs of the properties described in the seed data, and they must be
replaced with the real, authorised photography of each property before publication.

Source: Unsplash (https://unsplash.com), used under the Unsplash License
(https://unsplash.com/license). Files were downloaded and resized locally; no attribution is
required by the licence, but the source is recorded here for traceability.

Before launch: replace every demo image, keep alt text specific to the actual property, and
store private documents (owner IDs, mandates) outside the public media folder.
